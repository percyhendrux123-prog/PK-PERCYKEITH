# Clients — intake, onboarding, comms

The client journey, and the words we use at each step. Tighten this and both intake
and retention go up.

## Journey
1. **Lead** — saw content / referral / DM. Goal: book the convo.
2. **Intake convo** — understand the goal, the pain, the why. Goal: the right yes.
3. **Onboarding** — first 14 days set retention. Goal: a fast early win + belonging.
4. **Check-ins** — rhythm of accountability. Goal: progress + connection.
5. **Renewal / referral** — results → testimonial → referral loop.

## Principles
- Speed to lead matters — fast, human first reply beats a perfect slow one.
- Lead with their goal, not your program.
- Every message reinforces: *you belong here and you're making progress.*
