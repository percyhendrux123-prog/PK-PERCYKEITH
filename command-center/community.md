# Community — the PKfit Trainerize group chat

A strong group chat is the retention moat. People stay for results; they *stay longer*
for belonging. This is the engine to make the PKfit group the best room they're in.

## The feeling we're building
Strong, supportive, a little fun, zero judgment. People show up, get seen, and don't
want to let the group down. Percy is the heartbeat, not the lecturer.

## Weekly cadence (seed — tune to the group)
- **Mon — Intentions:** everyone drops one goal for the week.
- **Tue/Thu — Value/Coaching:** a tip, a myth busted, a quick win.
- **Wed — Win Wednesday:** celebrate a member's progress (with permission).
- **Fri — Check-in / accountability:** how'd the week go?
- **Weekend — Real talk / fun:** lighter, human, off-topic allowed. Bonds form here.
- **Always:** respond fast, name people, ask questions, spotlight effort over outcome.

## Engagement plays
- **Spotlight a member** weekly — recognition is the best retention tool there is.
- **Challenges** with a start/end and a shared scoreboard.
- **Prompts that demand a reply** (this or that, photo of today's meal/lift).
- **Rituals** — a recurring bit that's *theirs* (a hashtag, a Friday question).
- **Make heroes of the consistent**, not just the elite.
