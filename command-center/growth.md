# Growth — intake, retention, revenue

The math of the business. More of the right clients in, more staying, more value per
client — done in a way that still feels like PKfit.

## The three levers
1. **Intake** — more qualified leads → more booked → more closed.
2. **Retention** — keep clients longer (this is usually the cheapest growth).
3. **Revenue per client** — pricing, tiers, add-ons, done right.

## Intake
- Content engine feeds leads (`content.md`).
- A clear path: content → DM/CTA → call/form → close.
- Frictionless booking; fast human follow-up.
- Skills: `landing-page-generator`, `signup-flow-cro`, `form-cro`, `lead-magnets`,
  `free-tool-strategy`, `referral-program`.

## Retention
- The first 14 days + the community do the heavy lifting (`clients.md`, `community.md`).
- Watch for stall signals; re-engage early (`churn-prevention`).
- Results → testimonial → referral loop.
