# Content — slides, carousels, posts

Goal: a repeatable engine that turns Percy's ideas into scroll-stopping content we
**actually produce**, not just plan.

## What we make
- **Story slides** — vertical (1080×1920), punchy, one idea per slide.
- **Carousels** — IG/LinkedIn (1080×1350 or 1080×1080), hook → value → CTA.
- **Posts / captions** — in Percy's voice (see voice note below).

## The production loop
1. **Brief** — topic, audience, the one takeaway, the CTA.
2. **Hook** — 3 hook options, pick the strongest (`headline-psychologist`).
3. **Outline** — slide-by-slide beats, emotional arc (`emotional-arc-designer`).
4. **Copy** — tight, in voice (`copywriting`, `social-post-writer-seo`, `avoid-ai-writing`).
5. **Design + render** — produce the actual images:
   - HTML/CSS slide templates rendered to PNG, or
   - **Canva/Gamma** export (when connected), or
   - generated art via `imagen` / `fal-generate` / `fal-image-edit` / `stability-ai`.
6. **Ship** — deliver files; post (or hand to Trainerize/social when connected).
